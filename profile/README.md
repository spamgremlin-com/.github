# SpamGremlin 🛡️

### Telegram Moderation, Analytics & Growth Platform

**[SpamGremlin](https://spamgremlin.com)** is a platform built to help Telegram community owners, bot developers, and Mini App creators manage, analyze, and grow their Telegram presence.

Our goal is to bring essential Telegram management tools together in one place — making community administration easier, automation more accessible, and growth more measurable.

## 🚀 What We're Building

### 🛡️ Telegram Community Moderation

Tools designed to help administrators keep their communities safe, organized, and manageable.

- Anti-spam and anti-flood protection
- Anti-raid and automated moderation
- Community management and administrative tools
- Configurable moderation rules and workflows

### 📊 Telegram Analytics

Understand how your Telegram communities and bots perform.

- Community activity and engagement insights
- Bot interaction and event tracking
- Historical statistics and performance monitoring
- Data-driven insights for community growth

### 📈 Growth & Automation

A growing collection of tools for Telegram marketing, automation, and audience development.

- Telegram bot and channel management
- Campaign and communication workflows
- Link management and tracking
- Integrations and automation capabilities

### 🧩 Telegram Mini Apps Directory

We're also building a curated **Telegram Mini Apps directory** to make discovering useful Telegram applications easier.

The directory focuses on categorization, discoverability, and helping users explore the growing Telegram Mini Apps ecosystem.

Discover Telegram Mini Apps, explore different categories, and find new applications directly from the SpamGremlin platform.
